We are pleased to share a new preprint introducing **PhotoSVI**, a statistical framework for disentangling tonic and phasic dopamine signals in fiber photometry recordings.

Fiber photometry provides a powerful way to monitor neurochemical dynamics in vivo, but distinguishing changes in baseline activity from transient, event-related signals remains challenging. PhotoSVI addresses this by modeling both components within a unified framework, enabling more detailed analyses of dopamine dynamics across individual recordings and experimental cohorts. The approach also offers a way to investigate how pharmacological manipulations shape these distinct components of dopamine signaling.

This work brings together researchers from the **Bonaventura Lab** and the [Abante Lab](https://jordiabante.github.io/), combining expertise in neuropharmacology, fiber photometry, and statistical modeling.

**Read the preprint:** [Disentangling tonic and phasic dopamine in fiber photometry from single recordings to cohorts](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.16.767032v1)
