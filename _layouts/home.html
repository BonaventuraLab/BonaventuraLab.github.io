ABOUT THE LAB

From molecular mechanisms to behavior.

Our work focuses on the pharmacology and function of neurotransmitter systems, particularly dopamine and opioid signaling. We aim to understand how physiological and pathological brain states influence neurochemical signaling and, in turn, behavioral responses.

We combine molecular, circuit, behavioral, and imaging approaches to address these questions.
